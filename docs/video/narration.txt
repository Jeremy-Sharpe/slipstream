Most sales AI ends when the call summary appears. Slipstream starts there.

One buyer conversation writes the CRM, creates the safe follow-up, teaches the team who wins, and changes who they call next. This Revenue Loop is the entire product in one screen. The proof beside it comes from the deployed API: a mixed call-and-email cohort, a current ideal-customer profile, ten safe prospects, and a deliberately paused campaign.

The first input is a conversation, not a form. On this Northstar Labs call, Slipstream identifies the commercial signal, separates the speakers, and extracts the company, contact, deal stage, promises, objections, and agreed next step.

Every important claim remains attached to its source. Transcript turns and timestamps sit beside the proposed CRM update. A deterministic grounding pass checks model citations against the original words. Unsupported claims are repaired to the matching segment or dropped, so a fluent guess cannot quietly become CRM truth.

The output is not a blob of notes. It is a set of HubSpot-shaped company, contact, deal, task, and activity records that the rep can inspect before syncing. The same evidence produces a follow-up draft. Approval and delivery are separate states: this public demo can prove the workflow without pretending an email was sent.

Now the system learns across the whole sales motion. Intelligence compares won conversations with stalled and lost ones and derives the ideal customer profile from outcome-labelled CRM deals enriched by calls and email.

This is the original mechanic: Revenue DNA. Every profile carries a fingerprint of the evidence and outcomes that produced it. Watch a hypothetical new win arrive. The current profile becomes stale. Ten downstream leads require a new score. New sourcing pauses before provider spend. Relearning produces the next profile version. The interaction is explicitly a simulation, so the live CRM and campaign remain unchanged.

The current profile also exposes its evidence and the exact brief used for sourcing. Slipstream can send that brief to a provider, but this proof uses OpenRouter to generate fictional companies with reserved dot-example domains and no deliverable contact details. The ten results are embedded, ranked against the won-deal pattern, and tied to the profile version that scored them.

Campaigns closes the loop safely. This is a real API record, not a hard-coded success card. It has one queued draft, zero sends, zero attempts, a date in twenty ninety-nine, and a paused state. Production delivery remains disabled. With a provider configured, the worker claims bounded batches and distinguishes confirmed sends, safe retries, failures, and ambiguous results that need reconciliation.

Slipstream does not replace the CRM. It keeps the CRM current, makes every follow-up reviewable, and turns the team’s real conversations into the next best customer. The value scenarios shown here are transparent assumptions, not forecasts. A pilot replaces them with the customer’s baseline.

Conversation. CRM. Follow-up. Intelligence. Pipeline. One closed learning loop.
